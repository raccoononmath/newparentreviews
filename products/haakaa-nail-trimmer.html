haakaa Baby Nail Trimmer Review 2025

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.5/5 Rating
👶 Best for: 0-24 months 💰 We paid: $19.99

✨ Quick Verdict

We bought this to trim our baby's nails without the terror of clippers. It's been over 20 months, the battery is still original, nothing broke, and we'd buy it again without hesitation. Gentle, foolproof, and forgiving when baby moves.

👨‍👩‍👧 Why We Bought It

Growing nails. That's why. We didn't try anything else first — went straight to an electric trimmer because neither of us was comfortable using clippers on a newborn's tiny fingers.

👩 Mom says:

"It's very harmless. Baby can even touch the trimmer head when it's on and it won't injure anyone — it's that gentle. The trade-off is that gentle means it takes a bit longer to file the nails down compared to just clipping. But we chose the age-appropriate grinding pad which is the softest one, so it takes a little more time. That's a trade I'll take every time over accidentally nicking a finger. It comes with multiple pads for different ages — as baby gets older you use a slightly coarser one. Everything still works fine since mid-2024 and we haven't changed the battery yet."

👨 Dad says:

"If baby stays still, it does the job quickly. If baby moves around, it's harder — but that's not the trimmer's fault, that's just baby. The trimmer itself does its job perfectly. I haven't tried the adult pads on my own nails yet but Mom says it works for that too. The thing that sold me: you genuinely cannot hurt the baby with this even if something goes wrong. With clippers that's not true. We've had it since mid-2024 and nothing has broken, battery hasn't needed changing. For something we were nervous about, it's been completely stress-free."

📝 Slower Than Clippers — Worth It

Yes it takes longer. Clippers are faster. But clippers need a steady hand and a baby who stays still. One sudden move — you've cut skin. With this trimmer if baby moves, you just reposition and keep going. Nothing bad happens. Slower but zero risk of hurting baby. Easy trade-off for us.

The age-appropriate grinding pads are actually useful — different coarseness for newborn vs older baby. We've used the baby pads the whole time, work fine.

🔋 Battery — Still Original After 20+ Months

Still on the original battery since mid-2024. We use it every couple of weeks so that probably helps — not running it daily. But for a motorized tool that's been in use since mid-2024, we expected to have changed the battery by now. Haven't needed to. Pads are still holding up too.

⚖️ Pros and Cons

✅ What We Love

  • Physically cannot hurt baby — even if they move suddenly
  • Multiple age-appropriate grinding pads included
  • Original battery still going after 20+ months
  • Nothing has broken since mid-2024
  • Adults can use it too (different pads)
  • Zero stress compared to clippers

❌ What Could Be Better

  • Slower than clippers — takes patience
  • Harder when baby is squirmy (not the trimmer's fault)
  • Grinding pads will eventually need replacing

🎯 Final Verdict

Nervous about clippers on a newborn? Just get this. Slower, yes. Impossible to hurt baby, also yes. 20+ months in, original battery still going, nothing broke. Would buy again without thinking twice.
